# How quickly new jobs are found

Full article (figures as of 12 October 2026): https://jobspiq.in/blog/direct-ats-indexing-vs-job-board-noise

- "Discovery" is the moment Jobspiq's crawler first stored a job. Sources are re-checked about every 30 minutes.
- Measured only for sources that publish the exact posting time: 2,664 jobs posted in the 14 days to 12 October 2026.
- Median delay: Greenhouse about 6 minutes (107 jobs), Ashby about 12 (89), Hacker News about 36 (236), We Work Remotely about 1.3 hours (235), Himalayas about 2.5 hours (1,990). Lever was excluded (7 jobs).
- LinkedIn and Naukri publish a date but not a time, so their delay cannot be measured.

Limits: no claim is made about other job boards or about whether applying early helps.

Correction (12 October 2026): an earlier version claimed a "4x response advantage", a "24–72 hour" aggregator delay and a chart of average delays by job board. These were not measured and were removed.
