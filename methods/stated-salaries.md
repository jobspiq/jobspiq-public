# Employer-stated salaries

Full article (figures as of 12 October 2026): https://jobspiq.in/blog/understanding-tech-salary-benchmarks-india-2026

- Uses only salaries that employers state in the job posting. Jobspiq's own estimates are never included.
- Each salary is the midpoint of the stated range, in lakh rupees per year. Implausible values are removed.
- Groups with fewer than 25 stated salaries are not shown.
- In the 12 October 2026 article, 390 of 52,813 tech postings (0.74%) stated a salary, and 378 of those 390 were LinkedIn postings.

Limits: employers who state pay are not a random sample; samples are small; a stated range is what an employer advertises, not what anyone is paid.

Correction (12 October 2026): an earlier version of the article reported city-level medians and a stack-based pay premium that stated-salary data could not support. They were removed.
